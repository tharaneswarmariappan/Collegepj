        <h3><p>
            The ICC Cricket World Cup is one of the most prestigious international cricket tournaments in the world. It is played in the One Day International (ODI) format, where teams compete against each other to become the world champions. The tournament is organized by the International Cricket Council (ICC) and is held every four years. Over the years, the Cricket World Cup has produced some of the most memorable matches, legendary performances, and unforgettable moments in cricket history. The 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup was the tenth edition of the tournament and was hosted by <b><i>India, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh</i></b> from 19 February to 2 April 2011. Fourteen teams participated in the competition and played a series of group-stage and knockout matches. The tournament attracted millions of cricket fans from around the world and created a special atmosphere, particularly in the host countries. India entered the tournament with a strong squad featuring experienced players and talented young cricketers. Throughout the competition, India delivered impressive performances and progressed to the knockout stage. They defeated Australia in the quarter-final and Pakistan in the semi-final, setting up a final against Sri Lanka at the Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai.<i> In the final, Sri Lanka posted a challenging total, but India successfully chased it down with important contributions from Gautam Gambhir and captain Mahendra Singh Dhoni.</i> Dhoni's memorable six sealed India's victory and brought the World Cup trophy back to India after 28 years. With this achievement, India became the first team to win the Cricket World Cup on home soil. The 2011 World Cup remains an important chapter in ODI cricket and is remembered for its thrilling matches, remarkable individual performances, passionate fans, and <b>India's historic triumph.</b>
